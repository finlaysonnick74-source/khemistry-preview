About Khemistry

Connecting humans, in Melbourne and beyond.

Khemistry was founded by Maidlin Mistry after a 25+ year recruitment career across Australia, South East Asia and the UK. It was built from conversations that became genuine connections and long term partnerships.

We don’t advertise. We work with people we know, people they know, and through carefully curated direct approaches. We apply this approach to every role, from earlier career to C-suite.

Whether you’re exploring your next career move, hiring, or simply want to start a conversation, we’d love to hear from you.
